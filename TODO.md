## Shared-room party mode

A room code, a handful of players guessing the same animal, and a
scoreboard — the version of this that gets played at a party and
screenshotted afterwards. D1 and a working sync channel already exist
from Stream Mode (`functions/api/stream.js`, `stream.js`, `schema.sql`'s
`stream_state` table), which is the natural foundation for the room code
and scoreboard sync rather than building a new realtime layer from
scratch. Flagged in the growth plan as the highest-ceiling idea here and
also the one most likely to eat a month — do the cheap backlog items
first.

## Teachers outreach

Twenty questions is a classroom staple, a wet-break filler and a
car-journey standard. A teacher needs exactly what this site already is:
instant, honest answers about an animal, and a game that runs on the
whiteboard with no setup or login. The Daily fits a form-time slot almost
too neatly.

Where to look: r/Teachers, r/ScienceTeachers, primary teaching groups on
Facebook, TES — lead with the classroom use, not the URL.

Check first: AdSense is now live, and Google has extra obligations for
child-directed content — read the rules on child-directed treatment
before marketing the site at primary classrooms.

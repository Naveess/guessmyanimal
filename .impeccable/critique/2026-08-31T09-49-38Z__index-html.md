## Priority Issues

**[P1] The One-Yellow Rule is violated on real screens, not just in theory.**
- **Why it matters**: Screenshot-confirmed — the dice button, the "Did you know" fact card, and "Another animal" are all solid-Sun in a single scroll position on the animal page; the report dialog independently stacks a solid-Sun selected chip next to a solid-Sun Send button. The whole argument for Sun being "spent narrowly so it reads as a decision" collapses once three or four yellow blocks compete for attention in one fold.
- **Fix**: Give the fact card a tinted/outlined treatment instead of a solid fill (it's read-only content, not an action, so it doesn't need CTA-weight colour) — reserve solid Sun for the two things a user actually presses (dice, primary CTA). For the report dialog, give the selected "kind" chip an ink-outline or checkmark state instead of a solid Sun fill, so "Send report" is the one thing that owns the colour there.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P2] "At a Glance" still has no chunking on well-attributed animals.**
- **Why it matters**: Duck renders 8-9 unstructured chips in one wrapped block with no sub-grouping — the same shape of problem Quick Answers had before its `ans-gap` fix, just not yet applied here.
- **Fix**: Group by category (appearance/habitat vs. behaviour) with the same spacing-break treatment already proven on Quick Answers.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] The typed search query vanishes on selection with no trace.**
- **Why it matters**: `pick()` clears `#q` the instant a suggestion is chosen — for someone firing off rapid lookups mid-argument, there's no way to confirm what they searched for or recover from an accidental fuzzy-match pick except starting over.
- **Fix**: Consider leaving the picked animal's name in the field (a natural "you searched for X" confirmation) instead of clearing to empty, or briefly show the matched term before clearing.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] No distinct copy when both photo requests genuinely fail.**
- **Why it matters**: The pulse correctly stops when both requests settle with nothing found, but the resting state is visually identical to a a brief mid-flight moment for anyone not perceiving the animation (including reduced-motion users) — no text distinguishes "no photo exists" from "about to appear."
- **Fix**: A small, muted caption under the fallback emoji ("no photo on file") once both requests have genuinely settled empty.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] Wide-viewport emptiness reads as unfinished, not chosen.**
- **Why it matters**: At 1440px, roughly 60% of the screen is flat unused colour on either side of the content column. DESIGN.md frames this as a principled trade-off, and that's defensible — but nothing on screen signals the emptiness is a choice rather than a gap.
- **Fix**: Not a quick edit — needs a real compositional idea for wide viewports (texture, a secondary element, or an explicit "this is a phone-first product" statement) rather than a mobile layout that simply has more room around it.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

## Overall Impression

The three screens are clearly one considered system — reused components, consistent language, a real privacy story told in plain words at the one moment the site asks for a login. The gap isn't craft, it's pacing: all the reassurance is front-loaded onto the pitch screen and then evaporates completely at the two moments a streamer most needs it back — committing to "Go live" (an irreversible, whole-stream decision) and ending the stream (the flow's only destructive action, currently handed off to a bare native dialog).

## What's Working

1. **The You/Chat role split on the pitch screen** — a real answer to "what do I do, what does chat do," built from an existing primitive (`.party-hint-n`'s numbered badge) rather than a new icon language.
2. **The round timer's escalation** (`Muted → Warn → Bad` plus changing label text, "Hurry!") — state is legible without relying on color alone, exactly per the Do's/Don'ts rule.
3. **The `PLATFORMS` array pattern** — the inert "YouTube — Coming soon" row is structurally honest (no button at all) instead of a disabled control inviting a pointless tap.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] "End stream" depends on a native `window.confirm()`, which OBS Browser Source (Chromium Embedded Framework) frequently suppresses or never renders.**
Why it matters: DESIGN.md sells this exact surface as "capture it in OBS, a browser source" — but a synchronous JS dialog can silently fail to appear in that capture context, meaning the one control a streamer needs when they're done can do nothing for someone following the documented setup.
Fix: replace with an in-page confirm affordance (a two-step button, or an inline panel matching the report-dialog's existing language) instead of the browser's own dialog.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The Go-live screen gives no way back once connected, at the exact moment it's asking for the flow's biggest commitment.**
Why it matters: choosing a category here is "locked in for the whole stream" — a real, irreversible decision — communicated by one caption line on an otherwise nearly empty screen, with no disconnect/change-channel link and no restatement of stakes.
Fix: add a lightweight "not you? disconnect" link, and consider echoing back what's about to be locked in before the button is pressed.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] A failed chat join is a dead end.**
Why it matters: "Couldn't confirm channel's chat — check the channel name" names the problem but offers no retry — recovery requires a full page refresh and a fresh Twitch login.
Fix: add an inline "Retry connection" action next to the error message.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] All explanatory content is permanently one-way.**
Why it matters: the how-it-works panel and the You/Chat breakdown exist only on the pre-connect screen; nothing equivalent is reachable once live, so a streamer who forgets a mechanic mid-broadcast has nowhere to look.
Fix: a small persistent help affordance on the dashboard/live screen.
Suggested command: `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] No keyboard path for the two most-repeated live actions.**
Why it matters: "Reveal next hint" and "Next round" are pressed repeatedly during a live broadcast where a streamer's hands are typically already at the keyboard, yet both are mouse-only.
Fix: bind a key (e.g. Space or an arrow) to hint-reveal/round-advance while a round is live.
Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** No way to skip the pitch screen's five content blocks before connecting; no keyboard accelerators once live — every hint reveal and round advance is a dedicated mouse click. High friction for someone who already knows the product.

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Well handled pre-connect, then stranded immediately after — the Go-live screen assumes retained context ("Locked in... ending early is the only way to change it") with no link back to the explanation that justified it seconds earlier.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** A mistyped or suspended channel produces a status message with no recovery button; combined with the OBS `confirm()` risk, "End stream" and "reconnect chat" are the two weakest edges in the whole flow.

## Minor Observations

- The streamer's own leaderboard row and the round-winner state can coincide (`is-mine` + `is-winner`) and render correctly with no visual conflict.

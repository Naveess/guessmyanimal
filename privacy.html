Privacy

Short version: there's no account, nothing here is sold, and the only sign-in on the site is one streamers can choose to use. This page covers what the site itself stores, what its ads set, and how to reach the person who runs it.

What the site itself stores

Everything below stays on your device, in your browser's local storage - it is never sent anywhere, and there's no account for it to be attached to even if it were:

  • Your theme choice (light, dark, or system)
  • Mystery Animal progress - streaks, best score, which filter you last used
  • Whether you've seen the Daily intro before

If you send a problem report, what you typed and which animal it was about are stored so the issue can be fixed. No IP address, no cookie, no account, nothing that identifies who sent it.

Playing together

Party Mode and Twitch stream mode are the one place something is kept on the server rather than on your device, because several people have to see the same room: the room's code, the display names people chose, their scores, and the guesses they made. It lasts as long as the session does. There's no account attached to any of it, and a name is whatever someone typed.

Connecting a Twitch account

Streamers can connect their Twitch account so their channel name fills itself in. It's optional, it's only for the person running the stream, and it's the only sign-in anywhere on this site — anyone guessing in chat signs in to nothing.

It asks Twitch for no permissions at all. The site reads back which channel is yours, once, and throws the access token away in the same breath — nothing is saved, and there's no account to come back to. The only cookie involved is a random anti-forgery value that lasts five minutes, identifies nobody, and is deleted as soon as it's checked. Chat messages themselves are read the way anyone can read a public Twitch chat, without signing in to anything.

What the ads set

The site runs Google AdSense, which uses its own cookies to serve and measure ads. That's Google's activity on Google's terms, not something this site controls - their ad privacy policy covers what it involves and what choices you have over it.

If you're visiting from the UK or the EEA, you'll be shown a consent message before any personalised ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer below.

Get in touch

Wrong photo, wrong fact, a missing animal, or anything about how the site handles your data: the report form reaches the person who runs this directly, or open an issue on GitHub if you'd rather do that in the open.

The rest

The site is free, has no accounts, and is an early, ongoing project made for fun - expect it to keep changing. See About the game for how it works and why it exists.

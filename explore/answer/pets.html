    Animals people keep as pets

    "Commonly" means an ordinary pet you'd find in a pet shop or a neighbour's house. "Sometimes" means some people keep it somewhere, usually enthusiasts or where it's legal, like tortoises, tarantulas and pet pigs.

    Being kept as a pet isn't the same as being domesticated, and many animals on this list are still wild animals.

    93 animals, A to Z.

    Commonly kept as pets 24

    • 🐠AngelfishOne of the most commonly kept freshwater aquarium fish.
    • 🦎Bearded dragonOne of the most popular pet reptiles in the world, and the most kept reptile in the US in a 2021 study.
    • 🦜BudgerigarThe third most popular pet in the world, after dogs and cats: small, cheap and able to mimic speech.
    • 🐦CanaryKept as a pet for centuries, and bred into many coloured and singing varieties.
    • 🐠ClownfishPopular in the aquarium trade, and easily bred in captivity; the ocellaris is among the most traded marine fish.
    • 🦜CockatielOne of the five most popular pet birds; an estimated 4.8 million US households keep them, though many are given up within about 1.5 years.
    • 🦜CockatooPopular pets, but they can be difficult ones, and trapping for the pet trade has threatened some species.
    • 🐶DogThe first animal ever domesticated, over 14,000 years ago, before farming began.
    • 🐱Domestic catThe only domesticated member of the cat family.
    • 🦡FerretAn increasingly popular house pet: there are over five million in the United States alone.
    • 🐾FinchSeveral finches are common cage birds, above all the domestic canary.
    • 🦎GeckoThe leopard gecko is the most commonly kept, and the crested gecko is catching up.
    • 🐾GerbilThe Mongolian gerbil became a popular small pet in the English-speaking world after 1954.
    • 🐠GoldfishOne of the most popular aquarium fish, commonly kept in indoor tanks.
    • 🐹Guinea pigKept as pets worldwide, though first domesticated in the Andes for meat.
    • 🐠GuppyOne of the most popular freshwater aquarium fish.
    • 🐹HamsterThe golden or Syrian hamster is the most common pet; dwarf hamsters are kept too.
    • 🦎LizardMany species are kept as pets, including bearded dragons, iguanas, anoles and leopard geckos.
    • 🐭MouseThe fancy mouse, a domesticated house mouse, is a common pet, though less popular than some other rodents.
    • 🦜ParrotIntelligent and often able to imitate speech, which makes them popular pets.
    • 🐰RabbitEvery breed of domestic rabbit descends from the European rabbit, farmed for meat and fur and bred as pets.
    • 🐀RatFancy rats, bred from the brown rat, have been kept as pets since at least the late 1800s.
    • 🐾Stick insectAlmost 300 species have been reared as pets or in labs, and they have been pets since China's Han dynasty.
    • 🐠ZebrafishHardy, cheap and widely available, and considered good for beginner aquarists.

    Sometimes kept as pets 69

    • 🦙AlpacaA domesticated South American camelid bred for its highly valued fibre, descended from the wild vicuña at least 5,000–6,000 years ago.
    • 🐜AntMostly as ant farms, which were popular educational toys from the late 1950s to the late 1970s.
    • 🦎AxolotlAn occasional pet in home aquariums, and white and pink ones are common in the pet trade.
    • 🐝BeeHoney bees are kept by beekeepers for honey and pollination rather than as pets.
    • 🪲BeetleSome are kept as pets, such as diving beetles in a home freshwater tank.
    • 🐍Boa constrictorBoa constrictors are frequently kept and bred in captivity.
    • 🐸BullfrogAlbino bullfrogs are sometimes kept as pets, and the tadpoles are sold at fish stores.
    • 🐒Capuchin monkeyKept as pets in places, but the pet trade is one of the threats to wild capuchins.
    • 🐾CapybaraThe largest living rodent, a semiaquatic grazer found in every South American country except Chile.
    • 🐟CarpKoi, the colourful ornamental carp kept in ponds, were bred in Japan from the 1820s.
    • 🐟CatfishSmaller species such as Corydoras and plecos are popular aquarium fish.
    • 🦎ChameleonChameleons are popular reptile pets, mostly imported from Africa, though stricter rules on wild-caught animals have cut the trade.
    • 🐔ChickenKept mainly for meat and eggs, though some are kept as pets.
    • 🐾ChinchillaDomestic chinchillas, bred from the long-tailed chinchilla, are kept as pets, and most fur now comes from farms.
    • 🦀CrabMostly hermit crabs, which are commonly kept as pets and can live for 30 years.
    • 🦞CrayfishKept as pets in freshwater aquariums.
    • 🦗CricketKept as pets from China to Europe, sometimes for cricket fighting.
    • 🫏DonkeyMainly a working animal, though in richer countries a few are kept as pets or to guard livestock.
    • 🦆DuckDomestic ducks are raised for meat and eggs; a few are kept for show or as ornamental birds.
    • 🦅FalconKept and trained for falconry, hunting with captive birds of prey, rather than as pets.
    • 🦊Fennec foxBred commercially as an exotic pet, and kept as one as far back as ancient Rome.
    • 🐸FrogTailless, short-bodied amphibians, making up about 88% of all amphibian species.
    • 🐐GoatMostly livestock, but some people keep goats as pets for the close bonds they form.
    • 🦗GrasshopperPlant-eating insects with powerful hind legs for leaping, one of the oldest groups of chewing plant-eaters.
    • 🦅HawkTrained for falconry, which used to be called hawking, rather than kept as pets.
    • 🦔HedgehogThe pet kind is mostly the African pygmy hedgehog. Keeping a wild hedgehog is illegal in some countries, such as France and Italy.
    • 🦀Hermit crabCommon pets, often seen as throwaway ones, yet they can live 15 years or more; the record is 45.
    • 🐴HorseKept for riding, sport and work more than as pets.
    • 🦎IguanaThe green iguana is a popular pet, and it has been introduced well beyond its native range, including Florida and Hawaii.
    • 🪼JellyfishSoft, free-swimming sea animals with a bell-shaped body and stinging tentacles.
    • 🐾KinkajouSometimes kept as an exotic pet: playful and quiet, but it can occasionally be aggressive.
    • 🐠LionfishPopular aquarium fish, though they need an experienced keeper and are often sold to beginners who struggle with them.
    • 🦙LlamaA domesticated South American camelid, used by Andean cultures for meat and as a pack animal since before Columbus.
    • 🦜MacawMacaws are popular companion parrots, though several species are threatened in the wild.
    • 🐒MarmosetSometimes kept as pets, though their diet and housing needs are demanding.
    • 🐾MillipedeLarger millipedes, such as giant African species, are popular pets.
    • 🦎Monitor lizardMonitors are a staple of the reptile pet trade; the savannah monitor and Ackie dwarf monitor are the most commonly kept.
    • 🦎NewtSeveral species are kept as pets, including fire-bellied and paddletail newts.
    • 🐙OctopusEight-armed molluscs, about 300 species, with three hearts and copper-based blood.
    • 🦚PeacockSome people keep peafowl as pets.
    • 🐷PigFarmed mainly for meat, but some are kept as pets.
    • 🐦PigeonThe familiar city pigeon is the domestic form of the wild rock dove, native to southern Europe, North Africa and western Asia.
    • 🐟PiranhaSold as pets in some areas, but illegal in many parts of the US.
    • 🐸Poison dart frogKept by hobbyists, though collecting them for the pet trade has helped threaten some wild species.
    • 🐴PonyA type of small horse, with a thicker coat, mane and tail, shorter legs, heavier bones and a wider body than larger horses.
    • 🐾Prairie dogOnce taken from the wild for the exotic pet trade, but they are difficult pets needing constant care.
    • 🦗Praying mantisMantises are among the insects most commonly kept as pets.
    • 🐡PufferfishA few puffers are kept and even bred in home aquariums.
    • 🐍PythonMany species are sold in the exotic pet trade.
    • 🐾QuailThe king quail is often sold in the pet trade, where it is called a "button quail".
    • 🦎SalamanderTailed amphibians with slender, lizard-like bodies and moist skin, which keeps them near water or damp places.
    • 🦂ScorpionOften kept as pets, needing little more than a secure, lockable tank.
    • 🐠SeahorseKept by aquarium hobbyists, though wild-caught seahorses tend to do poorly in home tanks.
    • 🦐ShrimpSeveral kinds are kept in home aquariums, such as cherry and Amano shrimp.
    • 🦎SkinkBlue-tongued skinks were among the first lizards kept as pets and are still kept today.
    • 🦨SkunkSome people keep skunks as pets, usually with the scent glands removed.
    • 🐌SnailSnails are occasionally kept as household pets, though land snails are better known as garden and farm pests.
    • 🐍SnakeDocile species such as the ball python and corn snake are kept as pets, mostly captive-bred.
    • 🕷️SpiderEight-legged arachnids with fangs that inject venom and spinnerets that make silk.
    • 🐾Sugar gliderA popular exotic "pocket pet" in several countries, though it needs company to stay healthy.
    • 🕷️TarantulaIncreasingly popular as pets, though many New World species flick hairs that irritate skin.
    • 🐾TenrecThe lesser hedgehog tenrec is increasingly popular in the pet trade.
    • 🐢TerrapinWidely kept as pets in Europe, and there is an active pet trade in them.
    • 🐸ToadA common name for frogs with short legs and dry, warty skin, especially the true toads.
    • 🐢TortoiseOften kept as pets and famously long-lived; Adwaita, said to be Robert Clive's pet, was at least 150.
    • 🐸Tree frogAny frog that spends most of its life in trees.
    • 🐢TurtleSome land and freshwater species are widely kept as pets.
    • 🐾WoodlouseWoodlice are popular terrarium pets because of their varied colours and textures and how easy they are to care for.
    • 🐂ZebuSmall breeds such as the miniature zebu are kept as pets.

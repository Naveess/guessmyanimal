    Animals people keep as pets

    "Commonly" means an ordinary pet you'd find in a pet shop or a neighbour's house. "Sometimes" means some people keep it somewhere, usually enthusiasts or where it's legal, like tortoises, tarantulas and pet pigs.

    Being kept as a pet isn't the same as being domesticated, and many animals on this list are still wild animals.

    93 animals, A to Z.

    Commonly kept as pets 24

    • 🐠AngelfishOne of the most commonly kept freshwater aquarium fish.
    • 🦎Bearded dragonEight species of Australian lizards named for the spiny "beard" under the throat, which darkens and puffs up when they are stressed, threatened or courting.
    • 🦜BudgerigarA small, long-tailed, seed-eating parrot of Australia.
    • 🐦CanaryThe domestic form of the wild canary, a finch from the Macaronesian islands, which include Spain’s Canary Islands.
    • 🐠ClownfishSmall reef fish of the warm Indo-Pacific that live among sea anemones, sheltered by the stinging tentacles.
    • 🦜CockatielOne of the five most popular pet birds; an estimated 4.8 million US households keep them, though many are given up within about 1.5 years.
    • 🦜CockatooTwenty-two species of crested parrots from the Philippines and eastern Indonesia to New Guinea, the Solomon Islands and Australia.
    • 🐶DogThe first animal people domesticated, bred from wolves by hunter-gatherers more than 14,000 years ago, before farming began.
    • 🐱Domestic catThe only domesticated member of the cat family.
    • 🦡FerretA small domesticated member of the weasel family, most likely bred from the European polecat about 2,500 years ago.
    • 🐾FinchThe true finches: more than 200 species of small songbirds with stout, conical bills for cracking seeds and nuts, often brightly coloured.
    • 🦎GeckoNearly 2,000 species of small, mostly nocturnal lizards, the most species-rich lizard group.
    • 🐾GerbilAbout 110 species of small desert rodents from Africa, India and Asia, including jirds and sand rats.
    • 🐠GoldfishA small freshwater carp native to China, first bred for colour there over 1,000 years ago and now one of the most popular aquarium fish.
    • 🐹Guinea pigA domesticated rodent first bred for meat in the Andes as early as 5000 BC, and now a popular pet.
    • 🐠GuppyOne of the most popular freshwater aquarium fish.
    • 🐹HamsterStout-bodied rodents with cheek pouches that stretch to their shoulders, used to carry food home.
    • 🦎LizardScaly reptiles, usually with four legs, with over 7,000 species.
    • 🐭MouseSmall rodents best known through the house mouse, which lives alongside people almost everywhere.
    • 🦜ParrotAbout 410 species of intelligent, mostly tropical birds with strong curved beaks and grasping feet.
    • 🐰RabbitEvery pet and farmed rabbit belongs to one species, the European rabbit.
    • 🐀RatMedium-sized rodents, the best known being the brown and black rats that live alongside people.
    • 🐾Stick insectPlant-eating insects camouflaged as sticks or leaves, found on every continent except Antarctica, most of all in the tropics.
    • 🐠ZebrafishHardy, cheap and widely available, and considered good for beginner aquarists.

    Sometimes kept as pets 69

    • 🦙AlpacaA domesticated South American camelid bred for its highly valued fibre, descended from the wild vicuña at least 5,000–6,000 years ago.
    • 🐜AntSocial insects living in colonies of a queen, workers and males, with more than 13,800 species described of an estimated 22,000.
    • 🦎AxolotlA Mexican salamander that never goes through metamorphosis: adults keep their feathery external gills and stay in the water.
    • 🐝BeeFlying insects that feed on nectar and pollen, with over 20,000 species.
    • 🪲BeetleInsects whose front wings are hardened into wing-cases.
    • 🐍Boa constrictorBoa constrictors are frequently kept and bred in captivity.
    • 🐸BullfrogThe largest true frog in North America, named for the male’s call, which sounds like a bull bellowing.
    • 🐒Capuchin monkeyClever New World monkeys famous for their foraging skills, including using stones to crack open nuts.
    • 🐾CapybaraThe largest living rodent, a semiaquatic grazer found in every South American country except Chile.
    • 🐟CarpThe common carp is a large freshwater fish of lakes and rivers in Europe and Asia.
    • 🐟CatfishA huge, varied order of fish named for their whisker-like barbels.
    • 🦎ChameleonChameleons are popular reptile pets, mostly imported from Africa, though stricter rules on wild-caught animals have cut the trade.
    • 🐔ChickenThe domesticated form of the red junglefowl of Southeast Asia, first tamed around 8,000 years ago.
    • 🐾ChinchillaDomestic chinchillas, bred from the long-tailed chinchilla, are kept as pets, and most fur now comes from farms.
    • 🦀CrabArmoured crustaceans with the tail tucked under the body, able to run sideways.
    • 🦞CrayfishFreshwater relatives of lobsters, also called crawfish or crawdads.
    • 🦗CricketKept as pets from China to Europe, sometimes for cricket fighting.
    • 🫏DonkeyA domesticated member of the horse family, bred from the African wild ass some 5,000–7,000 years ago and used mainly as a working animal ever since.
    • 🦆DuckWaterbirds of the same family as geese and swans, but generally smaller and shorter-necked.
    • 🦅FalconAbout 40 species of fast birds of prey with thin, tapered wings, found on every continent except Antarctica.
    • 🦊Fennec foxThe smallest fox, from the deserts of North Africa.
    • 🐸FrogTailless, short-bodied amphibians, making up about 88% of all amphibian species.
    • 🐐GoatOne of the first animals ever domesticated, from the wild goat in Iran about 10,000 years ago.
    • 🦗GrasshopperPlant-eating insects with powerful hind legs for leaping, one of the oldest groups of chewing plant-eaters.
    • 🦅HawkBirds of prey of the eagle family, found on every continent except Antarctica.
    • 🦔HedgehogThe pet kind is mostly the African pygmy hedgehog. Keeping a wild hedgehog is illegal in some countries, such as France and Italy.
    • 🦀Hermit crabAbout 800 species of crustaceans that protect their soft abdomen in empty snail shells.
    • 🐴HorseA one-toed, hoofed grazer, domesticated on the steppes of western Eurasia.
    • 🦎IguanaThe green iguana is a popular pet, and it has been introduced well beyond its native range, including Florida and Hawaii.
    • 🪼JellyfishSoft, free-swimming sea animals with a bell-shaped body and stinging tentacles.
    • 🐾KinkajouA tree-dwelling relative of raccoons and coatis from the rainforests of Mexico and Central and South America.
    • 🐠LionfishPopular aquarium fish, though they need an experienced keeper and are often sold to beginners who struggle with them.
    • 🦙LlamaA domesticated South American camelid, used by Andean cultures for meat and as a pack animal since before Columbus.
    • 🦜MacawMacaws are popular companion parrots, though several species are threatened in the wild.
    • 🐒MarmosetTwenty-two species of tiny New World monkeys, most about 20 cm long.
    • 🐾MillipedeAbout 12,000 species of slow-moving arthropods with two pairs of legs on most body segments.
    • 🦎Monitor lizardMonitors are a staple of the reptile pet trade; the savannah monitor and Ackie dwarf monitor are the most commonly kept.
    • 🦎NewtOver 100 species of semiaquatic salamanders in North America, Europe, North Africa and Asia.
    • 🐙OctopusEight-armed molluscs, about 300 species, with three hearts and copper-based blood.
    • 🦚PeacockPeafowl are three species of pheasant: the Indian and green peafowl of Asia and the Congo peafowl of Central Africa.
    • 🐷PigA domesticated omnivore farmed mainly for pork.
    • 🐦PigeonThe familiar city pigeon is the domestic form of the wild rock dove, native to southern Europe, North Africa and western Asia.
    • 🐟PiranhaFreshwater fish of South American rivers and floodplains with razor-sharp teeth.
    • 🐸Poison dart frogOver 170 species of small, often brilliantly coloured frogs from the rainforests of Central and South America, active by day.
    • 🐴PonyA type of small horse, with a thicker coat, mane and tail, shorter legs, heavier bones and a wider body than larger horses.
    • 🐾Prairie dogFive species of burrowing ground squirrels from the grasslands of North America, named for their bark-like warning call.
    • 🦗Praying mantisMantises are among the insects most commonly kept as pets.
    • 🐡PufferfishFish that puff up into a ball when threatened.
    • 🐍PythonMany species are sold in the exotic pet trade.
    • 🐾QuailMid-sized game birds of two groups: Old World quail, related to pheasants, and New World quail in a family of their own.
    • 🦎SalamanderTailed amphibians with slender, lizard-like bodies and moist skin, which keeps them near water or damp places.
    • 🦂ScorpionArachnids with eight legs, grasping pincers and a curved tail ending in a venomous sting.
    • 🐠SeahorseSmall bony fish, 47 species, named for their horse-like head and neck.
    • 🦐ShrimpCrustaceans with long bodies, long antennae and thin legs, which swim forward with paddles under the tail and flick backwards to escape.
    • 🦎SkinkOne of the most diverse lizard families, with more than 1,500 species.
    • 🦨SkunkSome people keep skunks as pets, usually with the scent glands removed.
    • 🐌SnailSnails are occasionally kept as household pets, though land snails are better known as garden and farm pests.
    • 🐍SnakeLimbless reptiles with more than 4,170 species, found on every continent except Antarctica.
    • 🕷️SpiderEight-legged arachnids with fangs that inject venom and spinnerets that make silk.
    • 🐾Sugar gliderA small, nocturnal gliding possum named for its taste for sugary sap and nectar.
    • 🕷️TarantulaLarge, often hairy spiders, over a thousand species in almost 200 genera.
    • 🐾TenrecThe lesser hedgehog tenrec is increasingly popular in the pet trade.
    • 🐢TerrapinTerrapins are turtles of fresh or brackish water with clawed feet rather than flippers.
    • 🐸ToadA common name for frogs with short legs and dry, warty skin, especially the true toads.
    • 🐢TortoiseLand-living turtles with domed shells and column-like, elephant-like legs.
    • 🐸Tree frogAny frog that spends most of its life in trees.
    • 🐢TurtleShelled reptiles, about 360 species, including land-living tortoises, freshwater terrapins and sea turtles.
    • 🐾WoodlouseWoodlice are popular terrarium pets because of their varied colours and textures and how easy they are to care for.
    • 🐂ZebuSmall breeds such as the miniature zebu are kept as pets.

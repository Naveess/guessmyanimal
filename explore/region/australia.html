    Animals of Australia

    Australia is where the odd answers live: egg-laying mammals (platypus and echidna), pouched ones (kangaroo, koala, wombat, Tasmanian devil) and some of the world's most venomous snakes.

    It's a good list to know for the game, because so many of these animals break the usual rules.

    40 animals, A to Z.

    • 🐾BandicootAustralia and New Guinea, plus nearby islands such as the Bismarck Archipelago, Seram and Halmahera.
    • 🦎Bearded dragonThroughout much of Australia.
    • 🐾BilbyParts of northwestern and central Australia.
    • 🐾Bird of paradiseMainly New Guinea, plus eastern Australia and the Maluku Islands.
    • 🐾BlackbirdEurope, western Asia and North Africa; introduced to Australia and New Zealand.
    • 🐾BlobfishDeep water off mainland Australia, Tasmania and New Zealand, 600–1,200 m down.
    • 🦜BudgerigarAustralia.
    • 🐝BumblebeeMostly the Northern Hemisphere, north to Greenland; also South America and North Africa; introduced to New Zealand and Tasmania.
    • 🐾CassowaryNew Guinea, the Moluccas (Seram and Aru Islands) and northeastern Australia.
    • 🦜CockatielMainland Australia, mostly in arid or semi-arid country but always near water. It is largely nomadic, moving to wherever food and water are.
    • 🦜CockatooAustralia, New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, eastern Indonesia and the Philippines.
    • 🐾CraneEvery continent except Antarctica and South America.
    • 🐊CrocodileTropical rivers, lakes and swamps, and for the saltwater crocodile the coast, in Africa, Asia, Australia and the Americas. Unlike alligators, all crocodiles are sensitive to cold.
    • 🐕DingoAustralia.
    • 🐾EchidnaAustralia, including Tasmania, and New Guinea.
    • 🐾EmuMost of mainland Australia. The Tasmanian, Kangaroo Island and King Island forms died out after European settlement.
    • 🦇Fruit batTropical and subtropical Africa, Asia and Oceania; Papua New Guinea has the most species.
    • 🦘KangarooAustralia and New Guinea.
    • 🐨KoalaEucalyptus woodland in coastal eastern and southern Australia: Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria and South Australia.
    • 🐾KookaburraAustralia, New Guinea and the Aru Islands; the laughing kookaburra was introduced to New Zealand, Tasmania and Western Australia.
    • 🐾LungfishAfrica, South America and Australia; lungfish once lived all over the world.
    • 🐾LyrebirdEastern Australia: rainforests of Victoria, New South Wales and southeast Queensland; introduced to Tasmania.
    • 🦎Monitor lizardAfrica, Asia and Oceania, including New Guinea and Australia; the Nile monitor is invasive in South Florida.
    • 🐾MudskipperThe Indo-Pacific and the Atlantic coast of Africa. One widespread species runs from East Africa and Madagascar to Southeast Asia, northern Australia, southern China and Samoa.
    • 🐾NumbatSmall colonies in Western Australia, plus fenced reserves in South Australia and New South Wales.
    • 🦜ParrotMostly tropical and subtropical regions, with the greatest variety in South America and Australasia.
    • 🕷️Peacock spiderAll species live in Australia; the area around Cape Riche in Western Australia is a hot spot for them.
    • 🦆PlatypusRivers and streams of eastern Australia, including Tasmania.
    • 🐍PythonSub-Saharan Africa, South and Southeast Asia, southern China, the Philippines and Australia. Burmese pythons are invasive in Florida’s Everglades.
    • 🐾QuokkaSouthwestern Australia: Rottnest Island, Bald Island and scattered spots between Perth and Albany.
    • 🐾QuollMainland Australia, Tasmania and New Guinea, now in much smaller areas than before.
    • 🐾SawfishTropical and subtropical coasts, estuaries and rivers worldwide, but now gone from much of that range except northern Australia and Florida.
    • 🦂ScorpionEvery continent except Antarctica.
    • 🐾SeadragonSouthern Australian waters, from Wilson’s Promontory in Victoria west to Jurien Bay in Western Australia.
    • 🐾Sugar gliderCoastal forests of southeastern Australia; close relatives live across eastern and northern Australia and New Guinea.
    • 🦢SwanEurope, Asia and North America, plus the black swan of Australia.
    • 🐍TaipanAustralia and New Guinea.
    • 🐾Tasmanian devilTasmania only; it died out on the Australian mainland about 3,500 years ago.
    • 🦘WallabyAustralia and New Guinea; introduced to New Zealand, Hawaii and the UK.
    • 🐨WombatAustralia.

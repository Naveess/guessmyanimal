    Antarctic animals

    Very little lives on Antarctica itself. The animals that make it are the ones that feed at sea: penguins, seals, albatrosses, and the krill that much of the rest depends on.

    Polar bears are not on this list. They live only in the Arctic, at the other end of the world.

    4 animals, A to Z.

    • 🐾AlbatrossThe Southern Ocean and the North Pacific; absent from the North Atlantic.
    • 🐾KrillAll oceans; the Antarctic krill in the Southern Ocean.
    • 🐧PenguinAlmost entirely the Southern Hemisphere, from Antarctica to the Galápagos Islands at the equator.
    • 🦭SealOceans worldwide, mostly at sea but with a few species in fresh water, such as the Baikal seal.

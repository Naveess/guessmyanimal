    Scrubland animals

    Scrub and bushland sits between grassland and desert: dry country with low bushes and scattered trees. It's home to kudu and dik-diks, meerkats and mongooses, wombats and quokkas, roadrunners and tortoises.

    Many of these animals get much of their water from the plants they eat.

    Grassland animals

    Open grassland, savanna and steppe: the plains where the big herds graze and the predators follow them. Zebras, wildebeest, giraffes and gazelles in Africa, bison in North America, kangaroos in Australia, and the lions, cheetahs and hyenas that hunt them.

    Open country favours speed and good eyesight, which is why so many of the fastest runners are on this list.

    • 🐾AgoutiRodents related to guinea pigs but larger and longer-legged, living in forests and wooded areas of Central and South America.

    • 🐇HareLong-legged relatives of rabbits that live above ground and rely on speed, not burrows, to escape.

    • 🦅HawkBirds of prey of the eagle family, found on every continent except Antarctica.
    • 🦔HedgehogSmall, spiny insect-eaters of Europe, Asia and Africa that roll into a tight ball when threatened.

    • 🐾HornbillA family of birds of tropical Africa, Asia and Melanesia with a long, down-curved, often colourful bill, sometimes topped by a hollow casque.

    • 🐦HummingbirdThe smallest birds, about 375 species native to the Americas.
    • 🐺HyenaFour species of dog-like carnivores, including the spotted, brown and striped hyenas.
    • 🐾IbisTwenty-nine species of long-legged wading birds with long, down-curved bills, which they use to probe mud for food, usually in groups.
    • 🐾ImpalaA medium-sized antelope of eastern and southern Africa with a glossy reddish-brown coat.
    • 🐺JackalWild dogs of Africa and Eurasia: the black-backed and side-striped jackals of Africa and the golden jackal of south-central Europe and Asia.
    • 🐆JaguarThe biggest cat in the Americas and the only one of the roaring big cats native there.

    • 🦘KangarooThe largest marsupials, and the only large mammals that get around mainly by hopping on two legs.
    • 🦅KestrelThe common kestrel is a small falcon of open country across Europe, Asia and Africa, famous for hovering in one spot while it scans the ground.
    • 🦎Komodo dragonThe largest living lizard, a monitor lizard found only on a few Indonesian islands.
    • 🐞LadybirdOver 6,000 species of small, domed beetles, often red with black spots that warn predators they taste bad.

    • 🦁LionAfrica's social big cat.

    • 🦜MacawLarge, long-tailed and often colourful New World parrots in six genera.
    • 🐾MagpieThe Eurasian magpie is a black-and-white member of the crow family found across northern Europe and Asia, and one of the most intelligent birds.
    • 🐍MambaFour species of fast, highly venomous African snakes in the cobra family.
    • 🐾Maned wolfThe largest canine in South America, with a reddish coat and very long, slender legs.
    • 🐾MarmotFifteen species of large ground squirrels, the heaviest members of the squirrel family, from Asia, Europe and North America.
    • 🦫MeerkatA small, highly social mongoose of southern Africa.

    • 🐭MouseSmall rodents best known through the house mouse, which lives alongside people almost everywhere.

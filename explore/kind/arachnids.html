    Arachnids

    Eight legs and no antennae: spiders, scorpions, ticks and mites.

    The horseshoe crab is here because it's more closely related to spiders and scorpions than to real crabs.

    8 animals, A to Z.

    • 🕷️Black widowThe widow spiders: 34 species of small spiders with unusually potent venom.
    • 🐾Horseshoe crabNot a crab at all but a relative of spiders and scorpions, little changed for about 250 million years.
    • 🐾MiteTiny arachnids, mostly under 1 mm, with eight legs and a simple, unsegmented body.
    • 🕷️Peacock spiderA genus of tiny Australian jumping spiders, mostly 4–5 mm long.
    • 🦂ScorpionArachnids with eight legs, grasping pincers and a curved tail ending in a venomous sting.
    • 🕷️SpiderEight-legged arachnids with fangs that inject venom and spinnerets that make silk.
    • 🕷️TarantulaLarge, often hairy spiders, over a thousand species in almost 200 genera.
    • 🐾TickParasitic arachnids, related to mites, that feed on the blood of mammals, birds and sometimes reptiles and amphibians.

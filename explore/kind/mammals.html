    Mammals

    Nearly half the animals on the site are mammals. As well as the obvious ones, the list includes a few that catch people out in the game: whales, dolphins and seals (mammals that live in the sea), bats (mammals that fly), and the platypus and echidna (mammals that lay eggs).

    Humans are here too. Yes, you count.

    • 🐕African wild dogThe largest wild canine in Africa, with a patchy brown, black and white coat.
    • 🐾AgoutiRodents related to guinea pigs but larger and longer-legged, living in forests and wooded areas of Central and South America.

    • 🦫ArmadilloTwenty-one species of American mammals with a leathery, armoured shell and long digging claws.

    • 🐃BuffaloA large, heavily built wild cow of sub-Saharan Africa.

    • 🐾BushbabySmall nocturnal primates of sub-Saharan Africa, with big eyes, sharp hearing, bat-like ears and long tails for balance.

    • 🐾CoatiLong-nosed members of the raccoon family from the Americas.
    • 🐈CougarA large, plain-coated cat of the Americas, also called puma, mountain lion and panther.

    • 🐱Domestic catThe only domesticated member of the cat family.
    • 🫏DonkeyA domesticated member of the horse family, bred from the African wild ass some 5,000–7,000 years ago and used mainly as a working animal ever since.
    • 🐾DormouseAbout 28 species of small, nocturnal, tree-climbing rodents from Africa, Asia and Europe.
    • 🐪DromedaryThe one-humped camel, and the tallest of the three camel species.

    • 🐾ElandThe common eland, a huge spiral-horned antelope of the plains and open woodland of East and southern Africa.

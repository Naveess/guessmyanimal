    About Twitch's next screen

    Twitch's own authorization page may say this app can get your email address and whether it's verified. It can't — reading an email needs a Twitch permission called user:read:email, and this app never asks for it, so Twitch never sends it. That line is Twitch's standard wording, shown even to apps that request nothing — a known Twitch issue, not something this app requested.

    Your channel name, plus the names and guesses of people who play, for as long as the stream runs — deleted when you end it, and automatically after 48 hours either way. See the privacy page for the rest.

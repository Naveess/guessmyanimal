    About each

    Crayfish

    Freshwater relatives of lobsters, also called crawfish or crawdads. They live in streams, swamps, ditches and paddy fields, and most can’t tolerate polluted water.

    Everything on the crayfish page

    Lobster

    Large marine crustaceans with long bodies, muscular tails and big front claws, highly prized as seafood. They have blue blood, from copper-based haemocyanin.

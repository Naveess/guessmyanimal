    Beluga vs Narwhal: What's the Difference?

    Narwhals and belugas are close relatives, similarly built and known to interbreed; only the narwhal has a tusk.

    Common questions

    Are belugas and narwhals related?

    Yes. They are each other's closest relatives, similarly built and known to interbreed.

    Which one has a tusk?

    Only the narwhal. Males have a spiralled tusk 1.5–3 m long, which is an overgrown left canine tooth.

    About each

    Beluga

    An all-white whale of the Arctic and sub-Arctic, the narwhal’s closest relative. It has no dorsal fin, which helps it swim under ice, and a very bulbous melon on its forehead for echolocation.

    Everything on the beluga page

    Narwhal

    A toothed whale of the Arctic, closely related to the beluga. Males have a spiralled tusk 1.5–3 m long, an overgrown left canine tooth.

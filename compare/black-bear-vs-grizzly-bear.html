    About each

    Black bear

    The smallest and most widespread bear in North America, mostly living in forests. There are about twice as many as all other bear species combined.

    Everything on the black bear page

    Grizzly bear

    The North American brown bear, a population or subspecies of the brown bear. Some brown bears elsewhere, such as the Kodiak and Kamchatka bears, are sometimes also called grizzlies.

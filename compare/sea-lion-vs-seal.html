    Sea lion or seal?

    🦭Sea lionor🦭Seal

    How to tell them apart

    A sea lion has visible ear flaps and can walk on all four flippers. The true seals are the earless seals.

    About each

    Sea lion

    Eared seals with external ear flaps, long front flippers and the ability to walk on all fours. There are six living species, found from subarctic to tropical waters in both hemispheres.

    Everything on the sea lion page

    Seal

    Flippered marine mammals: the true seals, the sea lions and fur seals, and the walrus, 34 species in all. They eat at sea but come ashore to breed and rest.

    Dugong or manatee?

    🐾Dugongor🐾Manatee

    How to tell them apart

    Dugongs and manatees are the four living sirenians; the dugong lives in the Indo-West Pacific.

    The dugong is the manatee’s only living relative, found in the Eastern Hemisphere.

    About each

    Dugong

    A seagrass-eating marine mammal of the Indo-West Pacific, the manatee’s relative and the only living member of its family. Its closest relative, Steller’s sea cow, was hunted to extinction in the 1700s.

    Everything on the dugong page

    Manatee

    Three species of large, slow, fully aquatic plant-eating mammals, sometimes called sea cows, with paddle-shaped tails. They live in shallow coastal waters and rivers of the Caribbean, the Gulf of Mexico, the Amazon and West Africa.

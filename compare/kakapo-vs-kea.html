    Kakapo or kea?

    🐾Kakapoor🐾Kea

    How to tell them apart

    Both are New Zealand parrots, but the kākāpō is a heavy, flightless bird of the ground at night, while the kea flies and lives in South Island forests and mountains.

    About each

    Kakapo

    A large, nocturnal, ground-dwelling parrot found only in New Zealand, with mottled moss-green feathers and an owl-like face. It is the world's heaviest parrot and the only flightless one. Introduced cats, rats and stoats almost wiped it out; it now lives mostly on predator-free islands.

    Everything on the kakapo page

    Kea

    A large olive-green parrot of the forests and mountains of New Zealand's South Island, with bright orange under its wings. It is one of the few parrots that regularly spend time above the tree line, and it is famously curious and clever, solving puzzles and working in teams.

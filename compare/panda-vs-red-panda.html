    Panda or red panda?

    🐼Pandaor🐾Red panda

    How to tell them apart

    A red panda is a small reddish-brown animal with a ringed tail. It isn’t a bear, and it is more closely related to raccoons, weasels and skunks.

    The giant panda is a bear. The red panda is small, red and long-tailed, and not closely related, though both have a “false thumb” for gripping bamboo.

    About each

    Panda

    A black-and-white bear found only in the mountains of central China. It belongs to the carnivore order but lives almost entirely on bamboo, which makes up about 99% of its diet.

    Everything on the panda page

    Red panda

    A small, reddish-brown tree-climber of the eastern Himalayas and south-western China, with a ringed tail. Despite the name it’s closer to raccoons, weasels and skunks than to the giant panda, which is a bear.
